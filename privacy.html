Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

0day.story ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @0day.story to upload their own videos to TikTok through the TikTok Content Posting API.

What data the app uses

  • TikTok access tokens issued after the account owner authorizes the app via TikTok Login Kit.
  • Basic TikTok profile information (user.info.basic) needed to identify the account.
  • Video files and captions created by the account owner.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's own TikTok account. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. The app has no other users and collects no data from anyone else.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Locally stored tokens are deleted by the owner.
